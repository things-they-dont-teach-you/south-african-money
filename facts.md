# Facts and figures

**Applies to: figures checked on 2026-11-11.** The repo rate, and so the interest rate limits
that depend on it, can change at any Reserve Bank Monetary Policy Committee meeting. The next one
after this check is on 19 November 2026.

Every figure below comes from the linked official page or Act, on the date shown. Lessons point
here instead of repeating numbers, so updating this file updates the course.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |

## Maximum interest rates

Source: [National Credit Act Regulations: review of limitations of fees and interest rates](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201511/39379gon1080.pdf) (Government Notice 1080, Gazette 39379), checked 2026-11-11.
"RR" is the repo rate above. The last column is worked out from the repo rate on the check date.

| Type of credit | Maximum interest | At RR 7.25% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mortgage agreements (home loans) | RR + 12% a year | 19.25% a year |
| Credit facilities (credit cards, overdrafts, store cards) | RR + 14% a year | 21.25% a year |
| Unsecured credit (personal loans) | RR + 21% a year | 28.25% a year |
| Developmental credit (small business, low-income housing) | RR + 27% a year | 34.25% a year |
| Short-term credit (payday-style loans) | 5% a month on the first loan, 3% a month on later loans in the same calendar year | same |
| Other credit agreements | RR + 17% a year | 24.25% a year |
| Incidental credit (for example, late payment on an account) | 2% a month | same |

## Maximum initiation and service fees

Source: same regulations, checked 2026-11-11.

| Fee | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Initiation fee: unsecured, short-term, credit facilities and other credit | R165 plus 10% of the amount above R1 000, never more than R1 050 |
| Initiation fee: mortgage agreements | R1 100 plus 10% of the amount above R1 000, never more than R5 250 |
| Monthly service fee (all credit) | R60 a month |

An initiation fee may only be charged when a new credit agreement is made.

## Credit providers must register

Sources: [NCR: it is illegal for a credit provider to take a consumer's ID or cards](https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/latest-news/8-latest-news/121-it-is-illegal-for-a-credit-provider-to-take-a-consumer-s-id-or-cards-when-granting-credit) (October 2021) and the [National Credit Act 34 of 2005](https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act), sections 40, 89 and 90(2)(l), checked 2026-11-11.

- Anyone who lends money and charges interest must register with the NCR, whatever the amount or
  number of clients. This includes small lenders called loan sharks, mashonisas or skoppers.
- An agreement with a lender who should be registered but isn't is an unlawful agreement.
- A lender may not keep your ID, bank card, SASSA card or PIN. The NCR says doing so is a criminal
  offence: report it to the police and the NCR.
- All credit providers must stay within the maximum interest and fees above.
- Check registration: [NCR register of credit providers](http://www.ncr.org.za/register_of_registrants/registered_cp.php).

## Reckless lending

Source: [National Credit Act 34 of 2005](https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act), sections 80 to 83, checked 2026-11-11.

- A credit provider must assess whether you can afford credit before lending.
- Credit given without that assessment, or when it shows you can't afford it, can be reckless
  credit. A court can set aside all or part of a reckless agreement, or suspend it.

## Your credit record

Sources: [National Credit Act 34 of 2005](https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act), section 72; [NCR: challenge your credit record](https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/departments/education-and-communications?id=35), checked 2026-11-11.

| Right | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Free credit report | One free report from each credit bureau in any 12 months |
| Disputing wrong information | Complain to the credit bureau; it has 20 business days to resolve it |
| Reasons for refusal | If credit is refused, you can ask the credit provider for the reason |
| Paying to "clear" your name | Not needed: you never have to pay a third party to remove information from a bureau |

## Debt counselling

Source: [NCR: debt counselling](https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/departments/debt-counselling), checked 2026-11-11.

- Also called debt review. You apply voluntarily to an NCR-registered debt counsellor.
- The counsellor tells credit providers and credit bureaus within 5 business days.
- The application, negotiation and referral to court or the National Consumer Tribunal must be
  finished within 60 business days. During that time credit providers can't take legal action.
- While under debt counselling you can't take on new credit. A flag shows on your credit record
  (it is not a "blacklisting"). There is no payment holiday: you keep paying as advised.
- You exit with a clearance certificate once the restructured debts are paid (a home loan that is
  up to date may remain).
- Debt counselling is not free; the NCR publishes a fee guideline:
  [debt counselling fee structure guideline](https://www.ncr.org.za/documents/debt%20counselling%20fee%20structure%20guideline.pdf).
- Find a registered debt counsellor: [NCR register of debt counsellors](http://www.ncr.org.za/register_of_registrants/registered_dc.php).

## Limits on what you can owe after default

Sources: [National Credit Act 34 of 2005](https://www.gov.za/documents/national-credit-act), sections 103(5) and 129; [Prescription Act 68 of 1969](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201505/act-68-1969.pdf), section 11(d), checked 2026-11-11.

- **In duplum rule:** once you are in default, the interest, fees and charges that build up may
  not, together, be more than the unpaid balance at the time you defaulted.
- **Notice first:** before suing, a credit provider must send a written notice suggesting you
  go to a debt counsellor, ombud or dispute resolution agent (section 129).
- **Prescription:** most ordinary debts prescribe (can no longer be enforced) after three years,
  unless another Act says otherwise. The three years run from when the debt was due, and the
  clock can be interrupted if you acknowledge the debt or are sued.

## Deposit insurance

Source: [SARB: CODI frequently asked questions](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Deposit-insurance/CODI-Frequently-Asked-Questions), checked 2026-11-11.

- The Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI) covers qualifying deposits up to R100 000 per
  depositor per bank if a bank fails.

## Stokvels

Source: [Banks Act designation, Government Notice 620, Gazette 37903](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/37903gon620.pdf) (15 August 2014), checked 2026-11-11.

- A stokvel that meets the conditions in the notice is not doing "the business of a bank", so it
  does not need a banking licence.
- A stokvel that holds more than R100 000 in members' contributions must be a member of a
  self-regulatory body approved by the Reserve Bank, which issues it a certificate.
- It must keep accounting records and draw up yearly financial statements within 120 days after
  its financial year ends.

## Pyramid schemes

Source: [Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008](https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act), section 43, checked 2026-11-11.

- It is illegal to promote, or knowingly join or take part in, a pyramid scheme, a
  multiplication scheme or a chain letter scheme.

## Life and funeral insurance rights

Source: [FSCA: Life (long-term) insurance consumer guide](https://www.fscamymoney.co.za/Publications/Life%20(Long-term)%20Insurance%202021.pdf) (FSCA consumer education, 2020), checked 2026-11-11.

| Right | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cooling-off | You can cancel a life policy (including a funeral policy) within 31 days of receiving the policy documents, if you have not claimed |
| Grace period | At least 15 days after a missed premium before the policy lapses; some insurers give longer |
| Check the seller | The seller must be an FSCA-authorised financial services provider |
